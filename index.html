About Us

About Flying Horse Machinery Ltd

Flying Horse Machinery Ltd is an agricultural equipment company serving farmers and agricultural businesses with modern machinery and practical farming solutions.

Our range covers equipment for land preparation, irrigation, fodder processing, crop processing, harvesting and general farm operations. We are committed to making modern agricultural machinery more accessible to farmers while providing dependable service and support throughout the customer journey.
